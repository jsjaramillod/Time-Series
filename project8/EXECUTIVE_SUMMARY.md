# Common Signals and Forecast Accuracy

Sebastian Jaramillo · 25 November 2026

## Forecasting with many predictors

In my doctoral time-series assignment, compressing 100 predictors into two components gave the smallest one-step forecast error, 97.22. Re-estimating the three original methods recovered all three scores. Keeping all 100 predictors and shrinking their coefficients lowered the error to 95.60 on the same 100 outcomes, a 1.67% reduction. The strength of the penalty was selected on earlier observations.

## Interpreting the activity index

The activity factor recovered the original monthly persistence of 0.825. Correcting which series received eight income adjustments raised the estimated income loading from 0.206 to 0.323 with the same sampler. After the correction, the full fit estimated persistence of 0.811 with exceptional months replaced and 0.243 with all observed changes retained.

## A common market movement

In a separately documented sample of 4,126 trading days, market-factor loadings ranged from 0.88 for IWM to 0.99 for SPY. The funds tended to rise and fall together on the same day. The coursework preserved the figures but left the original price vintage and date range unverified.

## Hours after a productivity gain

Under the assumption that only technology permanently changes productivity, the assignment estimated a 98.25% chance that hours fell immediately after a favorable technology shock. The refit estimated 98.10% through 1994Q3 and 99.88% through 2019Q4. In the extended sample, the central 80% interval for the twelfth quarter includes zero.

## Scope of the improvement

The forecast gain is measured in the anonymous target’s units on a single panel. The activity and market estimates describe historical co-movement. The hours estimates give an economic interpretation conditional on the long-run restriction.
