# Forecast protocol

Use the supplied 1984Q1–2026Q1 snapshots of GDPC1 and T10Y2Y. The design fixes the first origin at 2003Q4, after 80 quarters. Each training window starts in 1984Q1 and sacrifices the initial four quarters for every model. Issue forecasts upon the first GDP publication for the last training quarter. Use observations through that quarter, even when part of the next quarter has elapsed. Score outcomes through 2026Q1 using this same GDP snapshot. The 2026Q1 origin is reserved for the fixed reference exercise. This is a revised-data, pseudo-real-time evaluation.

Compare random walk with drift and trend AR(4) at one and four quarters. Add four lagged Treasury spreads for a one-quarter ARDL. The primary sample retains all observed outcomes. From the 2022Q1 origin also compare the same models fitted to log GDP with eight intervening quarters interpolated linearly between 2019Q4 and 2022Q1. Score both on identical observed targets. For the overlapping four-quarter horizon, repeat scoring on each of the four disjoint origin phases. Report RMSE, central 90% coverage and width, fifth-percentile loss and breach frequency. No tuning by forecast score.

Use an equivalent change equation with a lagged level centered on the first observation and divided by 100, a linear trend in units of 100 quarters, and three lagged quarterly changes. Independent zero-mean normal prior standard deviations are 2 for the intercept, trend and scaled level, and 0.5 for each lagged change or spread. The drift benchmark uses the same intercept prior. The precision prior is Gamma with shape 2 and rate 1. One-step distributions and the drift benchmark integrate over parameter and innovation uncertainty. Use 65,536 posterior predictive paths for the four-quarter AR and independently check the fixed-origin posteriors with four Gibbs chains. Seed 20261118. Preserve all draws over the finite forecast horizon.
